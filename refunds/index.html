Your consumer rights

If you are a consumer in the EU, you may have a legal right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days. For a service that starts within that period at your request, you may be asked to pay a proportionate amount for what has been provided. These rights apply in addition to this policy and are not reduced by it.
