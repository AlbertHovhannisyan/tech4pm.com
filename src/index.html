About the training center

Built for product managers who work with engineers.

Most PM training stops at frameworks. Tech4PM teaches the technical material underneath: how software is structured, how data flows and how models are judged.

What graduates can do

Take part in design reviews

Read an architecture diagram, spot the risk and ask questions engineers take seriously.

Write specs engineers can build from

Describe APIs, data and non-functional needs precisely, without guessing.

Use data without waiting for an analyst

Query, check and question the numbers behind roadmap decisions.

Judge AI features on evidence

Set a quality bar, build an evaluation plan and decide when a model is ready to ship.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the courses for?

Product managers, product owners and aspiring technical PMs who work with engineering, data or ML teams. Each course page lists the level it assumes.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You learn to read and question technical material, not to build software. The SQL and Analytics workshop includes writing queries, and nothing else requires coding.

How much time does a course take?

Between four and ten hours a week depending on the course, listed on each course page. Sessions are designed around a full-time job.

How do I enroll?

Pick a course on the schedule page and submit an enrollment request. We confirm your place on the waitlist and send dates and the syllabus when the cohort opens.

Can my company train a whole team?

Yes. See the For teams page for private cohorts, workshops and custom curriculum.
