System Design for PMs

Learn how scalable systems are designed and how to take part in the trade-offs: latency, cost, reliability and complexity.

Syllabus

  1. Weeks 1–2
    Scale and latencyWhere time and money go as usage grows.
  2. Weeks 3–4
    ReliabilityFailure modes, redundancy and service levels.
  3. Weeks 5–6
    Queues and eventsAsynchronous work and why users see delays.
  4. Weeks 7–8
    Build, buy or integrateMaking and defending technical sourcing decisions.

Hands-on project

Review a real design document and write the product questions you would ask in the review.

You will be able to

  • Take part in design reviews
  • Explain trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders
  • Plan work that respects technical constraints
