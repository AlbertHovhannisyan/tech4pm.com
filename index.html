How training works

STEP 1

Learn the concept

Short live sessions on one technical idea at a time, taught from product situations.

STEP 2

Apply it to your product

Weekly exercises use your own systems, specs and data, not toy examples.

STEP 3

Finish with an artifact

Each course ends with a project you can use at work: a roadmap, a spec or an evaluation plan.
