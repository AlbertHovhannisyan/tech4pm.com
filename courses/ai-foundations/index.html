AI Foundations for Product and Project Managers

What language models can and cannot do, how to work with them safely, and where they save time in product and project work.

Syllabus

  1. Week 1
    How language models workPlain-language model of prompts, context and why answers vary.
  2. Week 2
    Prompting that holds upClear instructions, examples and asking for sources.
  3. Week 3
    Checking the outputSpotting confident errors and verifying against sources.
  4. Week 4
    Choosing tasks for AIWhich tasks to hand over, which to keep and which to review.

Hands-on project

Pick three recurring tasks from your own week, build a prompt for each and test them on real material.

You will be able to

  • Write prompts that give consistent results
  • Verify AI output before acting on it
  • Decide which tasks are safe to hand to AI
