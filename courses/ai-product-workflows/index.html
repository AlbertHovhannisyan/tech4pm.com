AI for Product Discovery and Specs

A hands-on workshop on using AI to synthesize research, draft and challenge specs and explore product data.

Syllabus

  1. Day 1
    Synthesizing researchCluster interviews and feedback, then trace every theme to its source.
  2. Day 1
    Drafting and challenging specsGenerate edge cases, acceptance criteria and engineering questions.
  3. Day 2
    Exploring dataAsk data questions in plain language and check the queries.

Hands-on project

Take one real feature from idea to a reviewed spec, with the AI critique and your decisions documented side by side.

You will be able to

  • Turn raw research into sourced themes
  • Use AI to find gaps in a spec before engineering does
  • Check AI-written analysis against the data
