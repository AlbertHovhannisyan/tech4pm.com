AI Risk, Privacy and Responsible Use

Set the rules for using AI at work: what data may be shared, how output is reviewed and how to respond when something goes wrong.

Syllabus

  1. Week 1
    Data and privacyWhat must never be pasted into an AI tool and why.
  2. Week 2
    Review and accountabilityWho approves what, and how to record it.
  3. Week 3
    Bias and errorsWhere models fail and how to test for it.
  4. Week 4
    Writing an AI use policyA short, usable policy for a team.

Hands-on project

Draft a one-page AI use policy for your team and test it against five real scenarios.

You will be able to

  • Classify data before using it with AI
  • Set review and approval steps for AI output
  • Write a team AI use policy people will follow
