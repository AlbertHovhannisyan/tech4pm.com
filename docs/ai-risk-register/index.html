Keeping a risk register current with AI

A risk register goes stale because updating it takes time. AI can draft the update from your weekly notes, so you spend your time on the decisions.

risk-update.prompt
input:  <weekly status notes + milestone plan>
task:   update the risk register
for each risk: description | likelihood | impact
                | owner | next action
rule:   mark any risk the notes do not support
        as an assumption

Review before you share

  • Check every risk against the source notes
  • Confirm owners with the people named
  • Remove anything you cannot defend in a steering meeting
You stay accountableThe register is your document. AI drafts it; you approve it.
