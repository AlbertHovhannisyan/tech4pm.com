Technical Foundations for PMs

The vocabulary and mental models behind software: front end, back end, databases, deployment and the trade-offs between them.

Syllabus

  1. Week 1
    How software is builtFront end, back end, services and how a release reaches users.
  2. Weeks 2–3
    Databases and storageWhat gets stored, where, and why it matters for features.
  3. Week 4
    Environments and releasesTesting, deployment and what makes a release risky.
  4. Weeks 5–6
    Working with engineersEstimates, trade-offs and how to ask a useful question.

Hands-on project

Document the architecture of a product you own and list its three biggest technical risks.

You will be able to

  • Explain your product architecture on one page
  • Understand engineering estimates and where they come from
  • Ask sharper questions in planning and reviews
