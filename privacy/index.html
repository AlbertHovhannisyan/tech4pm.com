1. Who we are

Tech4PM (“we”, “us”) runs this website and the Tech4PM training center. We decide how and why your personal data is used, which makes us the data controller. Legal entity: [legal entity name]. Address: [registered address]. Privacy contact: [privacy contact email].

2. What we collect

  • Enrollment and waitlist form: name, work email, whether you are a project or product manager, job title, years of experience, the course you chose, when you want to start and what you want to achieve.
  • Contact form: name, work email and your message.
  • Team proposal form: company, team size, your name, work email and your training goals.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Payment details: when you pay, our payment provider processes your payment. We receive confirmation of the payment and the amount, but never your full card number.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider may record your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and reliability.

Please do not include sensitive information, such as health details, in your messages. We do not ask for it.

  • To answer your questions and requests. Basis: our legitimate interest in replying, or steps you ask us to take before enrolling.

  • To take payment and keep payment records. Basis: performing our contract with you, and legal obligations such as accounting rules.
  • To send you resources and updates by email. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To keep the site secure and working. Basis: our legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as accounting rules, where they apply.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decisions that affect you.
