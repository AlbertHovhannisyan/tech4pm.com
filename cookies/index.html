Cookie Policy

What this site stores on your device

This site does not set cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking.

It uses your browser’s local storage for one thing: to remember the language you chose (Armenian or English) on the program pages, so you do not have to choose again. This is stored only on your device, is not sent to us and is not used to track you. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

Fonts from Google

To display text, the site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you open a page, your browser asks Google’s servers for the font files, so Google receives your IP address and browser details. This is not a cookie, but it is a transfer of data to a third party. See Google’s privacy policy for how it handles this data.

If this changes

If we add analytics or other tools that use cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Managing cookies and storage

You can block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser settings. If you clear the language setting, the program pages open in Armenian again.
